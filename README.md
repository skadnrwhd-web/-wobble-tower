# 🧱 Wobble Tower

A 3D block-pulling tower game that runs in your phone's browser. Real physics: friction, weight and collisions are all simulated.

## How to play
- **Drag a block** to pull it out. Go slowly. Rush it and the tower falls.
- Pull a block fully clear of the tower and let go. It is stacked on top automatically (+1).
- **Drag empty space** to rotate the camera. **Pinch** to zoom.
- 🎥 hides the on-screen buttons (for screen recording). 🔄 restarts.
- When the tower collapses, the round is over. Your best score is saved on your device.
